The queries a support bot should refuse to answer

An outbound engine for Indian BFSI: public complaint evidence, an agent that reviews and revises its own drafts, and a loop that rewrites its own instructions from its failure log — including where that backfired.

5,700 reviews · 19 apps 31 prospects · 5 verticals 9 drafts · 6 rejected by review Self-rewriting angle guidance
What this is built on

5,700 public Play Store reviews collected 20 Oct 2026 from 19 of 31 prospects (9 failed on wrong package IDs, 3 have no Android app). Only the 1,689 reviews carrying real text are scored — median review length in the raw corpus was 13 characters. Nothing here is estimated or simulated.

01

The thesis the data refused

This project was built to argue that Indian fixed-income platforms are drowning in tax and settlement queries that generic support bots answer wrongly. The engine was then pointed at real data to find the prospects where that was most visible. It did not find them.

Query classMentions in 5,700 real reviews

Zero TDS complaints. Seventeen hard-query mentions in total. The bond platforms the thesis was built around rank 10th and unscored, not 1st and 2nd.

Two readings fit, and this data cannot separate them: app reviews may simply be the wrong instrument — nobody writes a Play Store review about Form 15G, they raise a ticket — or the class is rarer than assumed. The honest position is that the hypothesis is untested, not confirmed. SEBI SCORES categorises complaints by type and is where this would actually be visible.

02

The finding that held

Three agent configurations against the same 18-case taxonomy of real fixed-income support queries. Read the first column, then the third.

ConfigurationAutomatedCorrectly disposedConfidently wrong

Naive retrieval and the integrated agent automate almost the same share of this query set. On a deflection-rate slide they are the same product.

One is correct 39% of the time with eight confidently wrong tax and settlement answers. The other is correct 94% with none. Routing alone is not the answer either — it is safe and automates 11%, which is not a product. The value is routing first, then integrating so the correct route is usually an answer.

03

Where the naive agent breaks

Every one of these is a tax, settlement or grievance answer delivered to an investor out of a generic help article, at high confidence. These are the ones that reach a grievance register, and not one of them appears on a deflection chart.

05

The outreach agent

A writer drafts from a closed research brief, a critic reviews it as a hostile reader, and rejected drafts go back with specific failures. The critic can hard-fail a draft outright — most importantly for any claim about the company that is not in the brief, which is the failure that would actually damage a sender.

Brief

Only measured facts. The writer knows nothing else about the company.

Write

One angle, one idea, under 130 words, no meeting ask.

Critique

Six hard fails plus five scored dimensions. Assumes the reader is looking for a reason to delete.

Revise

Back to the writer with named failures, up to three rounds.

The same prompts the Python agent uses. The writer drafts, an independent critic judges it against six hard fails, and a rejected draft goes back for revision.

06

The engine rewrites its own instructions

After each run the system reads its own rejection log, finds the failure mode that is dominant for one angle and not others, and rewrites that angle's guidance to pre-empt it. It proposes; a human applies; every change is versioned with the evidence behind it. Measured before and after, same prospects and same critic:

AngleGuidancenScoreShip rateRoundsTop first-round failure

It helped one angle and hurt the other. Evidence audit eliminated its target failure and went from a 40% to a 100% ship rate. One customer's words traded one failure for a new one: the rewrite's own example taught the writer to assert root causes the brief could not support, and the critic caught it on the first draft.

Self-improvement shifts failure modes as much as it removes them. That is the argument for keeping the reviewer independent of the thing being improved, and for proposing rather than auto-applying. Two attempts per evolved arm — the direction is clear, the magnitude is not.

07

What it sent

The drafts that survived review. Every figure traces to the brief; every quote is a real public review the recipient can find in ten minutes.

08

What this does not establish

Stated up front rather than discovered in questioning.

Nine prospects have no data

Their Android package IDs were wrong and returned 404, so nine BFSI firms — including Jiraaf, the second bond platform — are absent from the ranking entirely. Absence here is a collection failure, not a clean bill of health.

Reachability is barely assessed

Only company size band is filled. A named active leader and a warm path are null across all 31, which caps every confidence score at 0.78 and is the single biggest available improvement.

App reviews are a biased sample

Reviewers skew angry and resolved-badly, and only 30% of the raw corpus carried enough text to score at all. These shares are directional evidence of support strain, not a measure of contact volume or real resolution rates.

The eval scores structure, not tax law

It measures whether the agent routed correctly, not whether any substantive tax answer is right. The India-specific mechanics in the taxonomy are flagged for verification against current law.

Classification is keyword-based

Chosen for auditability: every verdict traces to a rule. Hindi and romanised Hinglish patterns were added after the first run found Devanagari complaints scored as neutral, then narrowed when generic abuse terms proved to inflate the channel-failure metric.

One run, no trend

A single snapshot. A support surface degrading month over month is a far stronger buying trigger than one that is merely bad, and nothing here measures change over time.
