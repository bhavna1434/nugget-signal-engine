Who the message is addressed to

A company is not a recipient. Each prospect carries a target record in one of three states, and the writer is constrained differently in each — because a role with no person attached is an invitation to invent one.

ProspectModeTargetWhy this role owns it

Ask any model to write personally to “the Head of CX” and it will produce something warm about their career and current priorities. All invented, all fluent, and the recipient is the one person who knows it is invented. So the critic carries a hard fail, invented_recipient, for any phrasing implying knowledge the record does not hold. A verified person needs every fact to carry a source URL; unsourced claims are dropped before the writer sees them, and the writer is told they were dropped.

Which format actually ships

All five formats were run. Two pass review; three do not. A format that cannot get a draft past an independent reviewer in three rounds is not a channel yet, whatever it looks like on a slide.

FormatChannelnScoreShip rateRounds used

The three that fail, fail differently. The LinkedIn note trips no hard fail at all — it is clean and simply scores 3.4 against a 3.6 bar, because it gives a VP of support no reason to care. The micro-page and the walkthrough script fail structurally and repeatedly on format_violated: over the word caps, wrong shape, “a spoken number dump that breaks the no-figures-the-listener-can-hold rule”.

One is a quality gap worth another round. The other two are constraints the writer cannot hit, which is a fault in how the formats were specified rather than in the drafts. Single runs each — enough to know they do not work, not enough to say by how much.

Formats are the second experiment dimension

Angles are what you lead with; formats are how it arrives. There is no reason to assume the angle that wins over email also wins in the post, so the log keys on angle × format and the question becomes measurable.

The phygital one

The engine rewrites its own instructions

After each run the system reads its own rejection log, finds the failure mode dominant for one angle and not others, and rewrites that angle's guidance to pre-empt it. It proposes; a human applies; every change is versioned with its evidence and is reversible.

AngleFormatGuidancenScore Ship rateRoundsTop first-round failure

It improved one angle and degraded the other. Evidence audit eliminated its target failure and went from a 40% to a 100% ship rate. One customer's words traded one failure for a new one: the rewrite's own worked example taught the writer to assert root causes the brief could not support, and the critic caught it on the first draft.

A self-improvement loop shifts failure modes as much as it removes them. That is the argument for keeping the reviewer independent of the thing being improved, and for proposing rather than auto-applying. Two attempts per evolved arm: the direction is clear, the magnitude is not.

What it changed, and why

Deflection rate hides the expensive failure

Three support-agent configurations against the same 18 real fixed-income queries, scored on whether each query was correctly disposed of rather than on how many were deflected.

ConfigurationAutomatedCorrectly disposed Confidently wrong

Naive retrieval and the integrated agent automate nearly the same share. On a deflection chart they are the same product. One is correct 39% of the time with eight confidently wrong tax and settlement answers; the other is correct 94% with none.

Routing alone is not the answer either — it automates 11%, which is safe and commercially useless. The value is routing first, then integrating so the correct route is usually an answer.

Where the naive agent is confidently wrong

Each is a tax, settlement or grievance answer delivered to an investor out of a generic help article, at high confidence. None of them appear on a deflection chart.

A hypothesis this data did not support

The engine was built to test a specific claim about where support pain concentrates in Indian fixed income. It is recorded here because the result changed what the engine prospects on.

The claim: that a material share of support volume sits in a query class — TDS and withholding, repayment and coupon timing, maturity and settlement, yield reconciliation — where the correct automated behaviour is to escalate, and that deflection-rate reporting therefore cannot see it.

What 5,700 reviews showed: zero TDS complaints and 17 hard-query mentions in total. Two readings fit and this data cannot separate them: app reviews may be the wrong instrument, since nobody writes a store review about a tax form — they raise a ticket — or the class is rarer than assumed. The honest position is untested, not disproved. SEBI SCORES categorises complaints by type and is where it would be visible.

The outreach consequently hooks on the support-channel signal, which is measured, rather than on the query class, which is not.
